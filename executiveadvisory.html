Executive Advisory

"The pace of business has changed. The way most businesses and leaders make decisions hasn’t. "

-Tony Chatman

Being a successful executive is no longer determined simply by experience, intelligence and strategy. It’s determined by your ability to make effective decisions when the unexpected hits and the path forward isn’t obvious. New technologies disrupt entire industries, customer expectations change constantly and markets shift overnight. Today leaders are expected to adapt faster than ever before. And the consequences of getting it wrong have never been greater.

The challenge isn’t simply leading more change. It’s making sound decisions while people, teams, and organizations respond to that change in very different ways.

Tony works with executives leading high-stakes change, where execution matters and failure is not an option. This is not coaching. This is a working partnership with someone who has spent 20 years working with more than 200 organizations across over 100 industries, diagnosing why change stalls, and providing the tools needed to get back on track.

Whether you’re navigating transformation, industry disruption, rapid growth, restructuring, or a strategic initiative that can’t fail, Tony will help you make better decisions when the stakes are the highest and the paths forward isn’t obvious.

Who This Is For

Executive Advisory is designed for CEOs, founders, presidents, and senior executives responsible for leading their organization through high stakes change, disruption, or complexity. When you’re responsible for decisions, where failure is not an option, you don’t need more opinions. You need Tony, a trusted thought partner who will help you lead with greater precision when it matters most.

HOW THIS STARTS

Apply and we’ll see if there is a fit. Not every applicant is accepted. This work requires a level of honesty and directness that not everyone is ready for. If this isn’t the right fit, Tony will tell you.

Together we’ll:

  • Separate symptoms from root causes.
  • Challenge narratives and ideas based on assumptions before they become expensive mistakes.
  • Evaluate issues through multiple perspectives.
  • Diagnose the root causes of resistance.
  • Build responses that move people and the organization forward.

This isn’t about having someone tell you what to do. It’s about having an experienced advisor who helps you determine what is actually true before you commit to the decisions that shape your organization.
